IT Companies

ZOHO

Zoho Corporation is an Indian multinational technology company that develops software products and cloud-based business applications. It was founded in 1996 by Sridhar Vembu and Tony Thomas. The company is headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

TCS

Tata Consultancy Services, commonly known as TCS, is one of the world's largest IT services and consulting companies. It is a part of the Tata Group and was founded in 1968. The company is headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

INFOSYS

Infosys Limited is one of India's leading IT companies. It provides technology, consulting, and digital transformation services to businesses around the world. The company was founded in 1981 and is headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
