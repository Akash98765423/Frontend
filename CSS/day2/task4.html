JANA NAYAGAN

Jana Nayagan is a Tamil political action drama directed by H. Vinoth and produced by KVN Productions. The film stars Joseph Vijay in the lead role alongside Pooja Hegde, Bobby Deol, and several other prominent actors. It was released theatrically on 23 July 2026.

HERO

The hero of Jana Nayagan is Joseph Vijay, popularly known as Thalapathy Vijay. He plays the lead role, named Vettri Kondan, in the film. Jana Nayagan is also notable for being promoted as Vijay's final film before focusing full-time on his political career

RELEASE-YEAR

The movie Jana Nayagan was released in theatres on 23 July 2026 in India.
